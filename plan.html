Pick reminders, then add the trip to Google Calendar or download an .ics for Apple Calendar, Outlook and others.

Wake-up:before sunrise
Google Calendar

Reminders are inside the .ics file. Google Calendar links can't carry reminders, so Google uses your default notification. “Before” reminders fire at 9 AM; wake-up is before sunrise at your spot (5 AM if no place is set). Made in your browser; nothing is sent.
